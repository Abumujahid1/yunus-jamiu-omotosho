About

01

I'm a detail-oriented Data Analyst with a background in internal audit, based in Lagos, Nigeria. I work at the intersection of two disciplines that both come down to the same question — does the data hold up? Auditing trains me to trace a number back to its source and question what doesn't reconcile; data analysis gives me the tools to turn that scrutiny into dashboards, trend reports and recommendations that hold up under review.

Day to day, that means cleaning and validating datasets, building reports in Excel and Power BI, writing SQL to pull and reconcile records, and translating findings into plain-language reports for management — whether the audience is an audit committee or a business team trying to understand what changed and why.
